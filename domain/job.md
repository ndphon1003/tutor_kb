# Job Domain

## Status

**Post-MVP product domain:** Job postings are explicitly outside the MVP.

No job-posting feature implementation is confirmed by this page. MVP implementation must not depend on a Job entity or job-posting workflow.

## Scope

This page will document the meaning, ownership, content, and lifecycle of a tutoring job posting once those decisions are made.

## To be defined

- Who may create, view, edit, close, or delete a posting
- Posting fields and validation
- Subject, level, location, schedule, and budget representation
- Draft, publication, closure, and archival lifecycle
- Discovery and visibility rules
- Relationship to applications and sessions
- Expiration and moderation rules

No job state machine or field schema is currently documented.
