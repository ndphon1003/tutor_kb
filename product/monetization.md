# Monetization

## Status

**Confirmed:** Registration and Tutor connections are free in the MVP.

Connection credits are **Planned post-MVP** and have no confirmed price or release date.

## Confirmed free MVP

- Tutor Seekers use tutor search, tutor details, AI recommendations, and connection requests without a platform fee.
- Tutors may register, create a profile, and submit it for approval without a platform fee.
- A Tutor Seeker sends a connection request to a Tutor.
- The Tutor reviews the request and accepts or rejects it.
- Accepting a request is free.
- Contact information is revealed to both parties after acceptance.
- Tutoring fees are not subject to a platform commission in the MVP because booking and lesson payment are not yet managed by the platform.

No payment or credit balance is required to register, publish an approved Tutor profile, send a request, accept a request, create a connection, or unlock contact information in the MVP.

## Planned post-MVP credit model

Connection credits may be introduced after the MVP. The intended future boundary is charging a Tutor when an accepted connection unlocks contact information.

Credit pricing, free allowances, packages, expiration, refunds, purchase flow, and rollout criteria are **TBD**. No previously discussed example price is an approved price.

## Contact-data protection

Before a connection is accepted, contact information remains private. The platform must prevent connection requests and profile content from exposing phone numbers, email addresses, Zalo identifiers, social-media handles, or other contact channels. This protects user privacy in the free MVP and preserves a future credit boundary.

## Future options

The following are possible future revenue models and are not confirmed MVP requirements:

- Connection credits
- Tutor subscription plans
- Clearly labeled sponsored profile placement
- Paid advanced AI features
- Commission on lessons after in-platform booking, payment, and lesson confirmation exist
